Table rates

Booking notifications

New bookings appear in the Supervisor's Bookings list the moment a player confirms — no setup needed. In the full version, the club can also get every booking as a WhatsApp message to the counter's number, the moment it is made — that hookup is part of the live build.

Who sees what

Customer — the public website: Book Now, Amenities, Shop, Services. No login, ever — the phone number is the key.
Supervisor — current tables and frame control, bookings, and only today's + this week's earnings.
Admin (owner) — everything: all earnings and history, the full Day Book, students, bookings and these settings.
Sign-in — the Login button (top right) opens a separate sign-in page that asks "staff or owner?": staff sign in with name + PIN and land on the Supervisor view; the owner signs in with email + PIN and gets full Admin.
Customer profiles — regulars are saved by mobile number only when they opt in; guests are never saved. A customer's name and number never appear on the public availability board.

Sign in

Are you the staff or are you the owner?

Pick one — staff run the counter, the owner sees and controls everything. Customers don't sign in at all: booking and records work with just a phone number.

Forgot your PIN? The owner resets staff PINs from Admin — it takes ten seconds. Every bill you save is tagged with your name.

Forgot your PIN? In the live version a reset code is emailed to this address — or use the one-time recovery code given at setup.

Demo sign-ins — Staff: any name, PIN 1234. Owner: any email, PIN 4321. In the live version the real PINs are set by the owner and checked on the server — they never appear in the page itself.

Customers: no login, no password, no account — your phone number is your key for bookings and your record. Customer accounts arrive with the Shop in version two, so anything you order there is saved under your account.
